DoveLingua's Course Structure

Seven levels. One clear path from zero to advanced.

DoveLingua English Learning Academy offers a complete English course structured across seven levels — from Pre-A1 for absolute beginners to C1 for advanced communicators. Every level is aligned with the CEFR, the international framework used by universities, employers and governments worldwide to measure English ability. At DoveLingua, you do not simply attend lessons — you build real communication skills, level by level, until you arrive.

The Reason Behind Level Duration

A real answer to a fair question.

“There are people on social media who promise English fluency in three months. Some promise less. These promises are not honest — and most students who have tried those methods know it.”

DoveLingua does not make those promises. What we offer instead is something far more valuable — a commitment to doing this properly.

The CEFR — the international standard our programme is built on — recommends approximately 100 to 200 guided learning hours per major level. DoveLingua delivers between 69 and 110 hours per level. We are not slow. We are honest.

🏗️

English is built, not downloaded

Each level builds on the one before. A student who rushes through A1 will struggle at B1. The time spent at each level is not wasted — it is invested.

🗣️

Communication takes practice

Reading about swimming and actually swimming are different things. You need time to practise speaking, listening, reading and writing — not just study rules.

🧠

The brain needs time to absorb

Language learning is not memorisation. It is rewiring how your brain processes communication. That cannot be rushed — but it can be made efficient, which is exactly what DoveLingua lessons do.

🎯

You arrive. You do not just attend.

At DoveLingua, you move to the next level because you are ready — because you passed the exam. The certificate you receive means something real.

DoveLingua Lesson Happenings

From the moment a lesson begins to the moment it ends.

Stage 1 — Encounter

You encounter new language through a real, meaningful context. You see it in use before you study it.

Stage 2 — Practice

You practise through speaking, listening, reading and writing. All four skills, every lesson.

Stage 3 — Real Use

You use what you learned independently — in a real conversation, to do something you could not do before this lesson.

⏱️

90 to 120 minutes per lesson

A DoveLingua lesson is significantly longer than a typical school lesson. This is deliberate — real communication cannot happen in 45 minutes.

🖥️

DoveLingua Classroom — not WhatsApp

Lessons take place on our dedicated professional platform — classroom.dovelingua.com. Students feel present, connected and focused.

📊

Assessment every two units

Every level includes regular unit tests and a final level exam. Students only progress when they are genuinely ready — not just because time has passed.

🌐

Online and in-person

Online students attend 3 lessons per week. In-person students attend 5 lessons per week in Maxixe, Inhambane, Mozambique.
