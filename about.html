Our Story

Born from observation

DoveLingua was not born from a business plan. It was born from a pattern observed too many times: students enrolling in English lessons with genuine motivation, studying hard - and then gradually disappearing. Not because they stopped wanting to learn. Because nothing told them they were going anywhere. Without a destination, the journey becomes impossible to sustain.

DoveLingua was founded to solve exactly that problem - to transform informal English learning into something structured, professional, and worthy of the effort every student invests.

Our Methodology

How we teach. Why it works.

DoveLingua follows the Communicative Language Teaching approach - the most respected methodology in modern English education. This directly addresses the most common failure of English teaching in Mozambique, where students study grammar for years but cannot hold a basic conversation.

Stage 1 - Encounter

Students encounter new vocabulary or grammar through a real communicative context. They see language doing something before they analyse it.

Stage 2 - Practice

Students practise through speaking, listening, reading and writing. All four skills are integrated in every lesson.

Stage 3 - Real Use

Students use the language independently - in real conversation, to do something they could not do before the lesson started.

The Programme

Seven levels. One clear path.

Every level is a complete learning experience with its own syllabus, communicative goals, assessments and certificate. Students progress upon passing a final exam, not simply by attending.

Level 0 Pre-A1

First Steps

For absolute beginners. No prior knowledge needed. Builds confidence and removes the fear of English before formal learning begins.

🕐 3 weeks
Level 1 A1

Beginner

Talk about yourself, family, daily life and surroundings. Foundation structures: verb to be, present simple, past simple.

🕐 ~12 weeks
Level 2 A2

Elementary

Handle everyday situations - shopping, food, past events and plans. First real experience of communicating in English.

🕐 ~13 weeks
Level 3 A2+

Pre-Intermediate

Express opinions, narrate events and discuss familiar topics. The bridge to genuine communicative competence.

🕐 ~14 weeks
Level 4 B1

Intermediate

Communicate with accuracy on a wide range of topics. The internationally recognised threshold of functional English.

🕐 ~12 weeks
Level 5 B2

Upper Intermediate

Communicate confidently on familiar and unfamiliar topics. Where English starts feeling like a real, flexible tool.

🕐 ~12 weeks
Level 6 C1

Advanced

Communicate fluently and precisely in complex academic, professional and social contexts. The completion of the DoveLingua journey.

🕐 ~12 weeks
